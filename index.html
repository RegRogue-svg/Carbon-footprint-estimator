What is your Carbon Footprint?

Many people don't realize just how much carbon their normal lifestyle takes a toll on our planet.

What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, primarily carbon dioxide, that are released into the atmosphere as a result of an individual’s, organization's, product's, or activity's actions. It measures the environmental impact of everyday activities, including transportation, electricity consumption, food production, and the manufacturing and purchasing of goods.

Why does your carbon footprint matter?

Understanding your carbon footprint is important because it allows you to recognize how your everyday activities and decisions contribute to climate change and environmental degradation.

Actions such as driving vehicles, consuming electricity, purchasing products, and producing waste all contribute to greenhouse gas emissions, which trap heat in the atmosphere and contribute to global warming.

By understanding your carbon footprint, you can identify areas where you have the greatest environmental impact and make more sustainable choices to reduce it. Even small individual changes, when adopted by communities on a larger scale, can collectively make a meaningful difference in protecting ecosystems, conserving natural resources, and creating a healthier environment for future generations.
